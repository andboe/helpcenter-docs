[#create-a-contract]
== Steps
1. Go to menu:Plan[Configuration].
2. Click btn:[Create].
3. Enter general details, see xref:#general[General].
4. Click btn:[Save].

The contract is shown. To ensure accurate scheduling, configure the following:

- xref:#work-time-guidelines[Work time guidelines]
- xref:#work-hours-per-day[Work hours per day]

[NOTE]
====
====
>
[NOTE]

[#work-hours-per-day]
=== Work hours per day
Work hours per day are used to ensure accurate calculation of paid absences such as vacation or sick leave.

==== Example

The person works 40 hours/week, 8 hours/day, with days off on Wednesday and Sunday.

To ensure correct hour distribution:

- Enter 8:00 for Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat
- Enter 0:00 for Wed, Sun

If the person takes sick leave or paid vacation, their working hours will be calculated based on these values.
Leaving a field blank will apply the default formula: `[Weekly Target Hours/Number of Working Days]`. This can lead to miscalculations because an even distribution of work hours across all working days will be assumed.

[#overtime-factors]
=== Overtime factors
Overtime factors are available to customers on the Enterprise WFM plan.

Overtime factors define how overtime hours are credited to the person's time accounts based on their contract, either for work on specific days or outside the opening hours.

As a prerequisite for receiving a credit for a specific day, the day type for which the credit is granted must be assigned to the employee’s primary planning unit on that day.

Credits are granted when the following conditions are met:

- An overtime factor exists in the employee’s contract for the day type.
- The day type is part of the planning calendar.
- The day type’s planning unit matches the employee’s primary planning unit on that day.
- If the credit is limited to a specific time period, the scheduled time falls within that range.

[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Field
|Description
|Day type
|The day type for which the overtime factor is applied.
|From
|Start date of time period.
|To
|End date of time period.
|Factor
|Multiplier (1.00 - 5.00) applied to hours worked during the specified period. For example, 1.00 credits hours at the standard rate, while 2.00 credits double time. Use a period (.) as decimal separator, e.g., 1.50.
|===

[#auto-scheduler-parameters]
=== AutoScheduler parameters
AutoScheduler parameters are used by the Create optimized schedule function, see xref:scheduling:schedules-job-optimization.adoc[Create optimized schedule].

[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Field
|Description
|Use target work times
|Uses the target work times to calculate weekly scheduled hours. If unchecked, the values under menu:Work time[Working hours per day] apply. See also xref:administration:concept-target-work-times.adoc[Calculate target work times].
|Schedule work after full day off
|Schedules a workday immediately after a full day off with activity type Vacation. For consecutive days off, a workday will be scheduled only after the last day off.
|Min. days off per week
|Minimum non-working days to be scheduled per week. Recommended if the planning unit operates on weekends.
|Min. consecutive days off per week
|Minimum consecutive non-working days to be scheduled in each week.
|Max. consecutive days off
|Maximum consecutive non-working days. This value is applied across weeks, not limited to a single week.
|Max. consecutive workdays
|Maximum consecutive working days. Recommended if the planning unit operates every day of the week. For example, if a person works 5 days per week, this parameter helps prevent scheduling them for 10 consecutive days.
|Min. workdays per week
|Minimum working days to be scheduled per scheduling week. Helps maintain a consistent staffing level in the planning unit to handle expected call volume.
|Weeks with max. 1 Sat work
|Number of weeks (1–5) forming a period during which a person can be scheduled to work one Saturday. Within this period, each person can be scheduled to work only one Saturday in total. Ensures fair distribution of weekend work and prevents the same people from consistently working Saturdays.
|Min. rest between shifts (h)
|Minimum rest period (in hours) between two consecutive shifts. Helps ensure compliance with labor laws on rest periods.
|===

[#scheduling-rules-and-parameters]
=== Scheduling rules and parameters
Scheduling parameters define contract-specific values that determine how scheduling rules are applied. For more details about scheduling rules and their status, see xref:administration:concept-scheduling-rules.adoc[Scheduling rules]
