2. Click the tab **Time off requested**.<br>You can filter the list by selections, planning units, recommendations, etc.

[#how-peopleware-displays-time-off-in-the-schedule]
=== How Peopleware displays time off in the schedule
Depending on what the [time-off valuation is based on](https://help.peopleware.com/understand-valuation), there may be differences in how Peopleware displays time off in the schedule:

- Valuation based on the schedule: All paid activities in the selected time range are replaced with the paid absence activity corresponding to the selected time off.
- Valuation based on the contract: By default, the beginning of their planning unit's business hours is the start of the time off in the schedule. This allows you to see how your approval affects the planning unit's coverage. For planning units without business hours, time off starts at midnight on the requested day.

[#full-day-requests-that-include-night-shifts]
=== Full-day requests that include night shifts
By default, full-day time off is interpreted as a full calendar day from midnight to midnight.
When valuation is based on schedules and a night shift crosses midnight, Peopleware checks the effective time range produced by the valuation and adjusts the full-day request so it matches the shift.
As a result, the request no longer overlaps with adjacent absences and planners do not need to edit start or end times.

Night shifts influence full-day time-off requests as follows:

- Night shift ends on the requested day: The effective start of the full-day request is moved to the minute the night shift finishes.
- Night shift begins on the requested day and ends the next morning: The effective end of the full-day request is moved to the minute that night shift finishes.
- Existing shifted requests carry over their adjustments to neighbouring full-day requests so that consecutive days line up without gaps or overlaps.

Example:
A person works from 10:00 PM on Monday to 6:00 AM on Tuesday and requests a full day off for Tuesday.
The system treats the full-day request as running from 6:00 AM on Tuesday to 6:00 AM on Wednesday.
A second request for Wednesday is automatically shifted to begin at 6:00 AM Wednesday, so the two full-day requests do not collide.

This automatic conflict resolution does not apply when the original time off was submitted as a part-day request.
Peopleware validates subsequent full-day requests against the exact requested hours and may block them if they overlap.

[#time-off-in-shift-center-and-me]
=== Time off in Shift Center and Me
Time Off and Shift Center do not exchange time-off data. Absences that are manually entered in Shift Center do not appear in Time Off or Me. They do not impact balances or the scheduling logic. This separation allows absences to be calculated consistently and avoids potential double-bookings.

To ensure consistency and correct valuation, add all absences via the Time Off app.

=== View the valuation breakdown

To view how each day of a time-off request was evaluated:

1. Go to {bc-time-off-requests}.
2. Click the **Time-off requests** tab.
3. In the list of requests, hover over the **To be booked** value in a request and click the {icon-svg-viewer}.
4. A detailed breakdown is displayed showing:
   - The valuation value for each day
   - The schedule-based or contract-based valuation strategy applied each day. The strategy for a time-off period may vary by day.

[#edit-the-valuation]
=== Edit the valuation
If you need to overwrite a calculated value or add a missing valuation, you can override the valuation. To do so, proceed as follows:

1. Go to {bc-time-off-requests}.
2. Click the **Time-off requests** tab.
3. In the list of requests, hover over the **To be booked** value in a request and click the {icon-svg-viewer}.
4. In the table, click the cell in the **Override** column corresponding to the time off that you want to edit.
5. Edit the value in the **Override** field.

Overriding the valuation has the following consequences:

- Peopleware does not adjust the time off in the schedule after the request has been approved. You must update the schedule manually.
- If you add an override to days that not did not have a valuation before, Peopleware does not add the time off to the schedule after the request has been approved. You must add the time off to the schedule manually.
- If xref:time-off:understand-valuation.adoc#why-can-the-hoursdays-to-be-booked-change-until-the-time-off-request-has-been-approved[Peopleware recalculates the **To be booked** value], you will see the recalculated value in the **Valuation** column, but Peopleware will not take it into account. You will have to update the schedule manually.

To delete an override, cancel the corresponding time off and add it again.

[#time-off-requests-for-night-shifts]
=== Time-off requests for night shifts
By default, when people request a full day off and you approve it, Peopleware inserts an absence of 24 hours into their schedule, provided that one of the following applies:

- They have requested an unpaid [type](https://help.peopleware.com/time-off-types)
- They have requested a paid type for which you have activated the [option to replace unpaid activities and fill up non-working hours](https://help.peopleware.com/time-off-types#replace-unpaid-activities-and-fill-up-non-working-hours).<br>For example, if a person requests to be off on 25&nbsp;June, their absence in Peopleware starts at midnight on 25&nbsp;June and ends at midnight on 26&nbsp;June, covering a total absence of 24&nbsp;hours.

The above applies to full-day requests without night shifts or if the person is not scheduled for the day requested.

However, full-day requests for days where the person is scheduled to work a night shift that spans two days (i.e. starts before and ends after midnight), are handled differently based on their schedule.

The following table explains different scenarios in which time off is requested for a period that includes a night shift:

[%header, cols="1a,1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Scheduled night shift
|Request
|Time off in schedule
|25&nbsp;June, 10&nbsp;PM until 26&nbsp;June, 6&nbsp;AM
|26&nbsp;June
|26&nbsp;June, 6&nbsp;AM until 27&nbsp;June midnight (18&nbsp;hours)
|25&nbsp;June, 10&nbsp;PM until 26&nbsp;June, 6&nbsp;AM
|25&nbsp;June
|25&nbsp;June, midnight until 26&nbsp;June 6&nbsp;AM (30&nbsp;hours)
|25&nbsp;June, 10&nbsp;PM until 26&nbsp;June, 6&nbsp;AM and 28&nbsp;June, 10&nbsp;PM until 29&nbsp;June, 6&nbsp;AM, not scheduled on 27&nbsp;June
|25&nbsp;June until 29&nbsp;June
|25&nbsp;June, midnight until 29&nbsp;June, 6&nbsp;AM
|===

Row&nbsp;3 shows how Peopleware can combine the variants from rows&nbsp;1&nbsp;and&nbsp;2. For requests that include a night shift at the beginning as well as at the end, the time off starts after the end of the first night shift and ends after the end of the last night shift.

[#staff-membership-changes]
== Staff membership changes
Changing the staff membership end date for a person affects their time-off requests.

If the staff membership end date lies before the requested period starts, the following applies:

- Approved time off is no longer displayed in {bc-time-off-plan}.
- For approved time off, the person's balance and time account are adjusted.
- For approved time off, quotas are adjusted accordingly.
- Pending time-off requests are rejected and removed from {bc-time-off-requests}.

If the staff membership end date falls within the requested period, the following applies:

- Approved time off remains visible in {bc-time-off-plan}.
- For approved time off, the person's balance and time account are adjusted for the days up to their staff membership end date. Time Off only values the part of the request that lies before the before the staff memberhsip end date.
- For approved time off, quotas are adjusted accordingly.
- Pending requests remain in {bc-time-off-requests} and need to be manually rejected.

People can still request time off that falls outside their staff membership.

[#balances-and-time-accounts]
== Balances and Time accounts
Balances and Time accounts are the two main ways to view and edit how much time off people have, and how much they have already taken.

[#time-accounts]
=== Time accounts
A time account is a ledger that keeps track of a person’s time-off. Each record represents an entitlement or time-off taken modification. These modifications can be either created automatically via entitlement policies and the request feature or manually. Time accounts display a balance for a defined period. Note that the balance for the displayed period will consider all previous records, regardless of whether they would expire in the meantime. You can create a time account record to modify the balance. Use time accounts to see a transparent history of how entitlement and taken time-off records affect the balance for a time type in a defined period.

[#balances-page]
=== Balances page
The Balances page displays an overview of real-time calculations of how much entitlement people in a planning currently have available for a time-off type and a planning unit. Balances also anticipate future expirations. The value already reflects upcoming reductions. They are always linked to the leave year of the time-off type, update dynamically based on Time account records and expiration rules, and are used in approval checks to project balances for cross-year requests. Use balances when you need to see what entitlement is currently usable, with upcoming expirations already considered, and can be exported for reporting (but not imported).

[#accounts-summary]
=== Accounts summary
Accounts summary is a reporting view that summarizes entitlement and taken time off for multiple people within a selected date range. The page displays totals per person and per time-off type. Account summary does not show record-level detail and does not anticipate future expirations. Use Account Summary when you need a high-level overview of granted and consumed time off across people.

[#frequently-asked-questions]
== Frequently asked questions
[#why-cannot-peopleware-restore-previous-shifts-when-i-cancel-time-off]
=== Why can Peopleware not restore previous shifts when I cancel time off?
When you approve a cancellation request or cancel approved time off for a person who had been scheduled to work on the days for which they then planned time off, you can restore their old shifts or clear their schedule. Peopleware cannot always restore previously scheduled shifts, and you only see the option **Clear**.

[NOTE]
====
Update schedules manually when shifts cannot be restored
====
>
[NOTE]
====
If you want the person to work on the days for which the time off is cancelled, xref:scheduling:schedules-edit.adoc[update their schedule manually in {bc-plan-schedules}].
====
Peopleware cannot restore scheduled shifts in the following cases:

- The time off was originally requested and approved in {bc-plan-time-off} instead of Time Off.
- The time off was [valuated with 0](https://help.peopleware.com/understand-valuation#why-do-i-not-see-a-valuation-for-a-time-off-request-) and was not added to the schedule.
- The time off was revaluated after approval because the person was assigned a new contract.
- The person has several part-day time off planned for the same day.
- The time range of the time off had been [edited by a planner](https://help.peopleware.com/time-off#edit-the-time-range-of-approved-time-off).
