Entitlement policies define the rules for automatically granting entitlement for a time-off type. Entitlement policies let you configure how much time off entitlement people receive, when it is granted, and when it expires.

Every entitlement policy applies to a leave year. A leave year is a recurring 12-month period that starts on a defined date (e.g. Jan 1). It defines how long entitlement is valid and when it expires.

You can set up entitlement policies that apply to everyone or only to people on specific contracts, e.g., different rules for full-time vs. part-time.

The entitlement strategy in the policy determines how time-off entitlement is granted for that leave year:

- **Yearly renewal**: Grants people a time-off entitlement for a whole year on a defined renewal date. Entitlement is valid for the leave year and expires at the end of it, unless carry-over rules extend the validity into the next year, with a configurable expiration date.
- **Accrual**: Builds entitlement gradually (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) until the end of the leave year. Accrued entitlement cannot be carried over. Any unused entitlement automatically expires at leave-year end.

The renewal date controls when entitlement is created and when people can begin requesting time off for the next leave year. Carry-over rules extend entitlement beyond the leave year.

[#create-an-entitlement-policy]
== Create an entitlement policy
1. Go to _Time Off > Types_{:.breadcrumbs}.
2. Select a time-off type.
3. In the **Entitlement policies** section, click **+ Add**.
4. Enter the basic information:
   - **Name**
   - (Optional) **Valid to** and **Valid from**.
   - (Optional) **Contracts**: Select the contracts to which the entitlement policy applies. To apply the entitlement policy to all people, do not select a contract.
5. Select an **Entitlement strategy**:
   - Select **Yearly renewal** to give people a fixed amount of time-off entitlement in the leave year period. Enter an **Entitlement amount** to define the number of days or hours a person receives under the entitlement policy. The **Renewal date** defines the day on which the entitlement policy creates a time-off entitlement. If you change this date after an entitlement has already been granted for the current leave year, Time Off creates a new entitlement record for the same leave year. This occurs if the new renewal date falls within that year and the previous entitlement was not yet active or was revoked.
   - Select **Accrual** to add time-off entitlement gradually (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) throughout the leave year. Enter an **Accrual amount** to define the number of days or hours a person earns at the end of each accrual cycle. Select an **Accrual frequency** to define how often entitlement is added. The first record creation typically occurs after 7 days for weekly accrual, 14 days for bi-weekly, or one month for monthly accrual.
6. Check the **Carry over** checkbox to allow unused time-off entitlement to be transferred into the next leave year. This option is available for Yearly renewal strategy only. Enter a **Max. carry over amount** to define the amount of unused entitlement that can be transferred from one leave year to the next. Enter a **Carry over expiration date** to define the date by which people must use carried-over time-off entitlement before it automatically expires.
7. Click _Save_{:.doc-button}.

[#edit-an-entitlement-policy]
== Edit an entitlement policy
1. Go to _Time Off > Types_{:.breadcrumbs}.
2. Select the entitlement policy you want to edit.
3. Adjust the details.
4. Click _Save_{:.doc-button}.

[NOTE]
====
====
>
[NOTE]
====
Changes are not applied retroactively.
====
[#delete-an-entitlement-policy]
== Delete an entitlement policy
1. Go to _Time Off > Types_{:.breadcrumbs}.
2. Select the time-off type from which you want to delete the entitlement policy.
3. Go to section **Entitlement policies**.
4. Click the {icon-delete} Delete icon on the row of the entitlement policy you want to delete.

[NOTE]
====
====
>
[NOTE]
====
Deleting an entitlement policy does not remove entitlement that was already granted under that policy.
====
