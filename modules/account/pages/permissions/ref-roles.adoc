Functional permissions that have corresponding object permissions are deactivated when setting object permissions, and vice versa. Example: Setting object permissions on individual dashboards deactivates the **View all dashboards** permission for the role.

|Allows people with the role to use all features in _Plan > Time off_{:.breadcrumbs}.

|Grants access to _Plan > Configuration_{:.breadcrumbs}. Access to configuration data types must be granted in _WFM > System > Role authorization > Navigator > Administration > Scheduling_{:.breadcrumbs}.

=== Time Off

[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Name
|Description
|View time off
|Grants view permissions on _Time Off > Requests_{:.breadcrumbs} and _Time Off > Plan_{:.breadcrumbs}.
|Create time off
|Allows people with the role to create time off.
|Edit time range
|Allows people with the role to edit the time range of approved time-off requests.
|Override evaluation
|Allows people with the role to override the automatically calculated evaluation.
|Cancel time off
|Allows people with the role to cancel approved time off.
|Recommend decision
|Allows people with the role to recommend a decision for time-off requests.
|Decide requests with recommendation only
|Allows people with the role to approve or reject only time-off requests that include a recommendation.
|Decide all requests
|Allows people with the role to approve or reject all time-off requests.
|Full access to Accounts
|Allows people with the role to access _Time Off > Accounts_{:.breadcrumbs}.
|View time-off quotas
|Grants view permissions on _Time Off > Quotas_{:.breadcrumbs}.
|Full access to time-off quotas
|Grants view and edit permissions on _Time Off > Quotas_{:.breadcrumbs}.
|View Configuration
|Grants view access to **Periods**, **Accounting rules**, and **Types**.
|Full access to Configuration
|Grants view and edit access to **Periods**, **Accounting rules**, and **Types**.
|===
