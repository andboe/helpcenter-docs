= What is Shift Center?

In {bc-plan-shift-center}, you can see and manage your *people*'s *shifts*.

Use *Shift Center* to do the following:

- xref:scheduling:analyze-coverage-staffing-requirement.adoc[Analyze coverage, requirement, and staffing]
- xref:scheduling:add-and-delete-activities-day-models-availabilities-and-comments.adoc[Add and delete activities, day models, and availabilities]
- xref:scheduling:modify-items.adoc[Edit activities, day models, and availabilities]
- xref:scheduling:find-replacement-employees-and-understaffed-shifts.adoc[Find a replacement person for activities and day models.]
- xref:scheduling:edit-or-delete-staff-requirements.adoc[Edit and delete staff requirements]
- xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#edit-the-generated-shifts[Edit generated shifts]

[#prerequisites]
== Prerequisites
To get the most out of the options available in *Shift Center*, make sure the following applies:

- You have created xref:administration:daymodel-creation.adoc[day models] and, if necessary, limited their usage in your xref:administration:create-and-manage-planning-units.adoc#limit-day-models[planning units].
- You have created at least one xref:administration:selections.adoc[selection].
- You have created at least one xref:people:advanced-filters.adoc[employee filter].
- You have configured your firewall to allow xref:getting-started:system-requirements.adoc#share-urls-for-websockets[WebSocket connections] for Peopleware. This is required for real-time updates.

[#action-bar]
== Action bar
The action bar at the top allows you to filter data and select the period for which you want to display data in the xref:#schedule-area[schedule] and xref:#parameter-area[parameter] areas.

[#filter-data]
=== Filter data
You have the following filtering options:

- Filter by *selection*: Click the {icon-filter-u} and use the drop-down menu on the left to select one.
- Filter by *employee filter*: Click the {icon-schedules-filter-employees-u} and use the drop-down menu on the left to select one.
- Filter by *level*: Click btn:[{icon-filter} Levels] and select the xref:scheduling:use-levels.adoc[levels] you want to include in the overview.

[#select-the-period-for-the-displayed-data]
=== Select the period for the displayed data
On the right side of the action bar, select the period in which you want to see your *schedules* as follows:

1. Select a start date with the date picker.
2. In the field next to the date picker, enter the number of time units (days, weeks, months, or years).
3. Select a time unit from the drop-down menu. This affects xref:#day-week-month-and-year-columns[how the table in the schedule area shows schedules].
4. {btn-apply}

[NOTE]
====
You can enter up to 371 days, 53 weeks, 24 months, or 2 years.
====
[#shift-center-areas]
== Shift Center areas
The *Shift Center* page includes the xref:#schedule-area[schedule area], the xref:#parameter-area[parameter area], and the xref:#message-area[message area]. The schedule and parameter areas consist of tables and display data relevant to scheduled *shifts*. The message area includes a message board.

The schedule and parameter areas include the *Level* column and the day, week, month, and year columns, which represent the settings configured in the action bar.

[#level-column]
=== *Level* column
The *Level* column displays rows with the *levels* you selected in the action bar.

[#day-week-month-and-year-columns]
=== Day, week, month, and year columns
Next to the *Level* column, you see the day, week, month, and year columns. The time unit you select from the drop-down menu in the action bar leads to a certain arrangement of the table:

- *Days*: The table displays expandable day columns for each day of the period. To expand a day column, click the corresponding header.  
  The day column expands to the right. The expanded day column's header has a 24-hour time scale at the top, and the rows show the *shifts* and *activities* throughout the day.  
  To collapse the expanded day column, click the day column's header. Use the arrow buttons on the left side of the time scale to change its size:
  - To increase the size of the time scale, click the right arrow.
  - To reduce the size of the time scale, click the left arrow.

- *Weeks*, *Months*, or *Years*: The table displays expandable week, month, or year columns, respectively. To expand a week, month, or year column, click the corresponding header.  
  The table displays expandable day columns that correspond to that week, month, or year, the xref:#contractual-time-actual-time-and-diff-columns[Contractual Time, Actual, and Diff. columns], and the xref:#time-account-column[Time Account column] at the right end.  
  To collapse the day columns, click the week, month, or year column's header.

[NOTE]
====
Empty day cells can have two different background shades. The shade indicates if *business hours* are configured:
====
>
[NOTE]
====
- Lighter shade: *Business hours* are not configured.
- Darker shade: *Business hours* are configured.
====
=== Resize areas

To resize an area, click the gray border between the areas and drag it up or down.

The following sections describe the *Shift Center* areas in further details.

[#schedule-area]
== Schedule area
The schedule area consists of the upper table and includes information about scheduled *shifts* and *activities*. The table displays information by *planning units* and the *people* assigned to them. In the schedule area, you can also xref:scheduling:add-and-delete-activities-day-models-availabilities-and-comments.adoc[add and delete activities, shifts, and availabilities] as well as xref:scheduling:modify-items.adoc[edit them].

[#planning-unit-column]
=== Planning Units column
The *Planning Units* column includes the *planning units* and the *people* assigned to them. To see the *people* assigned to your *planning units* and their *schedules*, click the {icon-plus} next to the *planning unit*. Each row includes the *person*'s name, their *personnel number*, and their *schedules* per *level* within the period you xref:#action-bar[set in the action bar].

By default, the day columns show color-coded *activities* with the abbreviation of the first *activity* in the *shift*. To change the data that the day cell displays, follow these steps:

- Right-click a day cell and hover over *Display*.
- Select one of the options:
  - *Scheduling data*: Displays the abbreviation of the first *activity* of the *shift*.
  - *Actual Working Hours*
  - *Time Account*
  - *Start Time*
  - *End Time*

If you expand the day columns, the table displays *activities* and *shifts* for each day in details.

  image::shift-center-overview/image-2.png[Day model in the schedule area, width=50%]

- *Variable day models*: A bar with a bottom border and dots at the bottom of the cell. The dots indicate possible *shift* times.

  image::shift-center-overview/image-3.png[Variable day model in the schedule area, width=50%]

[NOTE]

A gray day cell, *day model*, or *activity* means that the *person* is assigned to more than one *planning unit*, and the *day model* or *activity* is scheduled in another *planning unit*'s *schedule*.

[#parameter-area]
== Parameter area
The parameter area consists of the lower table with tabs at the bottom. It displays parameters from your scheduled *day models* and *activities*, e.g. *staff requirements*, *staffing*, and *coverage*.

In the *Day Models* and *Activity* tabs, the day cells display the total parameter value. +
The expanded day cells display the parameters in intervals throughout the day. By default, the expanded day cells display the parameters in 1-hour intervals. To view the parameters in 30- or 15-minute intervals, xref:#day-week-month-and-year-columns[increase the size of the expanded day cell's time scale].

The expanded day cells are color-coded as shown below. The more intense the color, the greater the difference between the *staff requirements* and the *staffing*.

   margin-left: 0px;

</style>

[%header, cols="1a,1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===

|*Understaffing*
|Fewer scheduled *people* than necessary
|Blue
|*Overstaffing*
|More scheduled *people* than necessary

In the parameter area, you can also xref:scheduling:find-replacement-employees-and-understaffed-shifts.adoc[find replacement for activities and day models], xref:scheduling:edit-or-delete-staff-requirements.adoc[edit and delete staff requirements], and xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#edit-the-generated-shifts[edit generated shifts].

The tabs at the bottom of the table provide different overviews of the parameters from your *people*'s *shifts*.

[#day-models-tab]
=== *Day Models* tab
The *Day Models* tab displays parameters from *shifts* created with *day models* used for the *planning unit*. To display the parameters of a *planning unit*, click the {icon-plus} next to the *planning unit* in the *Planning Units* column.

To select the parameters that you want to display, right-click a day cell or an expanded day cell and select one of the options:

- *Coverage*: Difference between the number of assigned and required *shifts*.
- *Requests*: Number of *shifts* that *people* requested.
- *Staffing*: Number of *people* scheduled.
- *Requirement*: The *shift requirements* created by the xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#generate-shifts[Shift generation functionality].

[#activities-tab]
=== *Activities* tab
The *Activities* tab displays parameters from *activities* by *planning unit*. To display the parameters of a *planning unit*, click the {icon-plus} next to the *planning unit* in the *Planning Units* column.

To select the parameters that you want to display, right-click a day cell or a cell in the expanded day column and select one of the options:

- *Requirement*: Number of *people* necessary for the *activity*.
- *Staffing*: Number of *people* scheduled.
- *Coverage*: The difference between the number of scheduled and required *people*.
- *Net maximum capacity*: Number of scheduled *people* who have the necessary *skills* for the *activity*.
- *Staffing according to shift requirement*: Number of scheduled *people* if all *shifts* from the xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#generate-shifts[Shift generation functionality] are scheduled.
- *Coverage according to shift requirement*: *Coverage* if all *shifts* from the xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#generate-shifts[Shift generation functionality] are scheduled.

[#activity-overview-tab]
=== *Activity Overview* tab
The *Activity Overview* tab displays parameters from a single *activity* or *activity type* by *planning unit*. To display the parameters of a *planning unit*, click the {icon-plus} next to the *planning unit* in the *Planning Units* column. The table displays the period as xref:#action-bar[set in the action bar] in columns and 15-minute interval rows.

To select the *activity* or *activity type*, the parameter, and the *level* that you want to display, right-click a day cell and hover over one of the options:

- *Activity*: Select an *activity* or *activity type*.
- *Parameter*
- *Level*

[#message-area]
== Message area
The message area consists of the bottom area with a board. The message area displays warning and error messages when scheduling rules are violated. To display or hide the message area, press *CTRL + K* or right-click any day cell in the schedule area to open the context menu. Then, select *Message window*.

To delete a message, click the message and press *Delete*. You can also select multiple messages.

To delete all messages, press *CTRL + A* and then press *Delete*.
