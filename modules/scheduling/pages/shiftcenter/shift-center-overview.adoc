= What is Shift Center?

:author:

:description: Learn what Shift Center is used for and how to customize the display of data.

In _Plan > Shift Center_{:.breadcrumbs}, you can see and manage your _people_{:.css}'s _shifts_{:.css}.

Use _Shift Center_{:.label} to do the following:

- xref:scheduling:analyze-coverage-staffing-requirement.adoc[Analyze coverage, requirement, and staffing]
- xref:scheduling:add-and-delete-activities-day-models-availabilities-and-comments.adoc[Add and delete activities, day models, and availabilities]
- xref:scheduling:modify-items.adoc[Edit activities, day models, and availabilities]
- xref:scheduling:find-replacement-employees-and-understaffed-shifts.adoc[Find a replacement person for activities and day models.]
- xref:scheduling:edit-or-delete-staff-requirements.adoc[Edit and delete staff requirements]
- xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#edit-the-generated-shifts[Edit generated shifts]

[#prerequisites]
== Prerequisites
To get the most out of the options available in _Shift Center_{:.label}, make sure the following applies:

- You have created xref:administration:daymodel-creation.adoc[day models] and, if necessary, limited their usage in your xref:administration:create-and-manage-planning-units.adoc#limit-day-models[planning units].
- You have created at least one xref:administration:selections.adoc[selection].
- You have created at least one xref:people:advanced-filters.adoc[employee filter].
- You have configured your firewall to allow xref:getting-started:system-requirements.adoc#share-urls-for-websockets[WebSocket connections] for Peopleware. This is required for real-time updates.

[#action-bar]
== Action bar
The action bar at the top allows you to filter data and select the period for which you want to display data in the xref:#schedule-area[schedule] and xref:#parameter-area[parameter] areas.

[#filter-data]
=== Filter data
You have the following filtering options:

- Filter by _selection_{:.css}: Click the {icon-filter-u} and use the drop-down menu on the left to select one.
- Filter by _employee filter_{:.css}: Click the {icon-schedules-filter-employees-u} and use the drop-down menu on the left to select one.
- Filter by _level_{:.css}: Click _{icon-filter} Levels_{:.doc-button} and select the xref:scheduling:use-levels.adoc[levels] you want to include in the overview.

[#select-the-period-for-the-displayed-data]
=== Select the period for the displayed data
On the right side of the action bar, select the period in which you want to see your _schedules_{:.css} as follows:

1. Select a start date with the date picker.
2. In the field next to the date picker, enter the number of time units (days, weeks, months, or years).
3. Select a time unit from the drop-down menu. This affects xref:#day-week-month-and-year-columns[how the table in the schedule area shows schedules].
4. Click _Apply_{:.doc-button}.

[NOTE]
====
====
>
[NOTE]
====
You can enter up to 371 days, 53 weeks, 24 months, or 2 years.
====
[#shift-center-areas]
== Shift Center areas
The _Shift Center_{:.label} page includes the xref:#schedule-area[schedule area], the xref:#parameter-area[parameter area], and the xref:#message-area[message area]. The schedule and parameter areas consist of tables and display data relevant to scheduled _shifts_{:.css}. The message area includes a message board.

The schedule and parameter areas include the _Level_{:.label} column and the day, week, month, and year columns, which represent the settings configured in the action bar.

[#level-column]
=== _Level_{:.label} column
The _Level_{:.label} column displays rows with the _levels_{:.css} you selected in the action bar.

[#day-week-month-and-year-columns]
=== Day, week, month, and year columns
Next to the _Level_{:.label} column, you see the day, week, month, and year columns. The time unit you select from the drop-down menu in the action bar leads to a certain arrangement of the table:

- _Days_{:.label}: The table displays expandable day columns for each day of the period. To expand a day column, click the corresponding header.  
  The day column expands to the right. The expanded day column's header has a 24-hour time scale at the top, and the rows show the _shifts_{:.css} and _activities_{:.css} throughout the day.  
  To collapse the expanded day column, click the day column's header. Use the arrow buttons on the left side of the time scale to change its size:
  - To increase the size of the time scale, click the right arrow.
  - To reduce the size of the time scale, click the left arrow.

- _Weeks_{:.label}, _Months_{:.label}, or _Years_{:.label}: The table displays expandable week, month, or year columns, respectively. To expand a week, month, or year column, click the corresponding header.  
  The table displays expandable day columns that correspond to that week, month, or year, the xref:#contractual-time-actual-time-and-diff-columns[Contractual Time, Actual, and Diff. columns], and the xref:#time-account-column[Time Account column] at the right end.  
  To collapse the day columns, click the week, month, or year column's header.

[NOTE]
====
====
>
[NOTE]
====
Empty day cells can have two different background shades. The shade indicates if _business hours_{:.css} are configured:
====
>
[NOTE]
====
- Lighter shade: _Business hours_{:.css} are not configured.
- Darker shade: _Business hours_{:.css} are configured.
====
=== Resize areas

To resize an area, click the gray border between the areas and drag it up or down.

The following sections describe the _Shift Center_{:.label} areas in further details.

[#schedule-area]
== Schedule area
The schedule area consists of the upper table and includes information about scheduled _shifts_{:.css} and _activities_{:.css}. The table displays information by _planning units_{:.term} and the _people_{:.css} assigned to them. In the schedule area, you can also xref:scheduling:add-and-delete-activities-day-models-availabilities-and-comments.adoc[add and delete activities, shifts, and availabilities] as well as xref:scheduling:modify-items.adoc[edit them].

[#planning-unit-column]
=== Planning Units column
The _Planning Units_{:.label} column includes the _planning units_{:.css} and the _people_{:.css} assigned to them. To see the _people_{:.css} assigned to your _planning units_{:.css} and their _schedules_{:.css}, click the {icon-plus} next to the _planning unit_{:.css}. Each row includes the _person_{:.css}'s name, their _personnel number_{:.css}, and their _schedules_{:.css} per _level_{:.css} within the period you xref:#action-bar[set in the action bar].

By default, the day columns show color-coded _activities_{:.css} with the abbreviation of the first _activity_{:.css} in the _shift_{:.css}. To change the data that the day cell displays, follow these steps:

- Right-click a day cell and hover over _Display_{:.label}.
- Select one of the options:
  - _Scheduling data_{:.label}: Displays the abbreviation of the first _activity_{:.css} of the _shift_{:.css}.
  - _Actual Working Hours_{:.label}
  - _Time Account_{:.label}
  - _Start Time_{:.label}
  - _End Time_{:.label}

If you expand the day columns, the table displays _activities_{:.css} and _shifts_{:.css} for each day in details.

=== _Time Account_{:.label} column
The _Time Account_{:.label} column displays the xref:administration:concept-target-work-times.adoc[target work times].

[#parameter-area]
== Parameter area
The parameter area consists of the lower table with tabs at the bottom. It displays parameters from your scheduled _day models_{:.css} and _activities_{:.css}, e.g. _staff requirements_{:.css}, _staffing_{:.css}, and _coverage_{:.css}.

In the _Day Models_{:.label} and _Activity_{:.label} tabs, the day cells display the total parameter value.<br>The expanded day cells display the parameters in intervals throughout the day. By default, the expanded day cells display the parameters in 1-hour intervals. To view the parameters in 30- or 15-minute intervals, xref:#day-week-month-and-year-columns[increase the size of the expanded day cell's time scale].

The expanded day cells are color-coded as shown below. The more intense the color, the greater the difference between the _staff requirements_{:.css} and the _staffing_{:.css}.

[%header, cols="1a,1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Color
|_Coverage_{:.css}
|Description

|_Understaffing_{:.css}
|Fewer scheduled _people_{:.css} than necessary
|Blue
|_Overstaffing_{:.css}
|More scheduled _people_{:.css} than necessary
|Green

|The number of scheduled _people_{:.css} matches the _staff requirements_{:.css}.

In the parameter area, you can also xref:scheduling:find-replacement-employees-and-understaffed-shifts.adoc[find replacement for activities and day models], xref:scheduling:edit-or-delete-staff-requirements.adoc[edit and delete staff requirements], and xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#edit-the-generated-shifts[edit generated shifts].

The tabs at the bottom of the table provide different overviews of the parameters from your _people_{:.css}'s _shifts_{:.css}.

[#day-models-tab]
=== _Day Models_{:.label} tab
The _Day Models_{:.label} tab displays parameters from _shifts_{:.css} created with _day models_{:.css} used for the _planning unit_{:.css}. To display the parameters of a _planning unit_{:.css}, click the {icon-plus} next to the _planning unit_{:.css} in the _Planning Units_{:.label} column.

To select the parameters that you want to display, right-click a day cell or an expanded day cell and select one of the options:

- _Coverage_{:.label}: Difference between the number of assigned and required _shifts_{:.css}.
- _Requests_{:.label}: Number of _shifts_{:.css} that _people_{:.css} requested.
- _Staffing_{:.label}: Number of _people_{:.css} scheduled.
- _Requirement_{:.label}: The _shift requirements_{:.css} created by the xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#generate-shifts[Shift generation functionality].

[#activities-tab]
=== _Activities_{:.label} tab
The _Activities_{:.label} tab displays parameters from _activities_{:.css} by _planning unit_{:.css}. To display the parameters of a _planning unit_{:.css}, click the {icon-plus} next to the _planning unit_{:.css} in the _Planning Units_{:.label} column.

To select the parameters that you want to display, right-click a day cell or a cell in the expanded day column and select one of the options:

- _Requirement_{:.label}: Number of _people_{:.css} necessary for the _activity_{:.css}.
- _Staffing_{:.label}: Number of _people_{:.css} scheduled.
- _Coverage_{:.label}: The difference between the number of scheduled and required _people_{:.css}.
- _Net maximum capacity_{:.label}: Number of scheduled _people_{:.css} who have the necessary _skills_{:.css} for the _activity_{:.css}.
- _Staffing according to shift requirement_{:.label}: Number of scheduled _people_{:.css} if all _shifts_{:.css} from the xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#generate-shifts[Shift generation functionality] are scheduled.
- _Coverage according to shift requirement_{:.label}: _Coverage_{:.css} if all _shifts_{:.css} from the xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc#generate-shifts[Shift generation functionality] are scheduled.

[#activity-overview-tab]
=== _Activity Overview_{:.label} tab
The _Activity Overview_{:.label} tab displays parameters from a single _activity_{:.css} or _activity type_{:.css} by _planning unit_{:.css}. To display the parameters of a _planning unit_{:.css}, click the {icon-plus} next to the _planning unit_{:.css} in the _Planning Units_{:.label} column. The table displays the period as xref:#action-bar[set in the action bar] in columns and 15-minute interval rows.

To select the _activity_{:.css} or _activity type_{:.css}, the parameter, and the _level_{:.css} that you want to display, right-click a day cell and hover over one of the options:

- _Activity_{:.label}: Select an _activity_{:.css} or _activity type_{:.css}.
- _Parameter_{:.label}
- _Level_{:.label}

[#message-area]
== Message area
The message area consists of the bottom area with a board. The message area displays warning and error messages when scheduling rules are violated. To display or hide the message area, press _CTRL + K_{:.label} or right-click any day cell in the schedule area to open the context menu. Then, select **Message window**.

To delete a message, click the message and press _Delete_{:.label}. You can also select multiple messages.

To delete all messages, press _CTRL + A_{:.label} and then press _Delete_{:.label}.
