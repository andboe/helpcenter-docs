:page-aliases: adherence

{bc-intraday-real-time-adherence} compares *people*'s *scheduled activities* with their *actual activity* in real time. When *people* track their work in an *external system*, e.g. an ACD, for which you have configured an xref:acd-integration:how-integrations-work.adoc[integration], Peopleware imports their *status data*, and compares it with their *schedule* in Peopleware. In menu:Real-Time Adherence[] you can see if *people* work in adherence with their *schedule* and take timely action when they deviate from it.

[#prerequisites]
== Prerequisites
- You have configured the xref:acd-integration:import-agent-status-data.adoc[real-time agent status import] for the *external system* whose data you want to compare with your *schedules* in Peopleware.
- You have allowed *Real-Time Adherence* to open a xref:getting-started:system-requirements.adoc#share-urls-for-websockets[WebSocket connection], which is required for real-time updates. If the WebSocket connection cannot be opened or needs to be recreated due to firewalls or an unstable network connection, you see a notification.
- You have added the relevant external identifiers to your *people* in Peopleware.

For more adherence details, map external activities to activities in Peopleware. To define a more detailed *mapping*, use xref:intraday:adherence-matches.adoc[Matches].

[#adherence-statuses]
== Adherence statuses
In {bc-intraday-real-time-adherence}, each person has a status based on the comparison between their scheduled and their actual activity:

[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Status
|Description
|In adherence
|The type of the scheduled activity matches the type of the actual activity. The row is not highlighted.
|*Out of adherence*
|The type of the scheduled activity does not match the type of the actual activity. The row is highlighted in red.
|===

[NOTE]
====
If you have not configured an integration and mapped *external identifiers*, the following applies:
====
>
[NOTE]
====
- People who are scheduled appear as *in adherence*, and *Offline* is displayed as their actual activity. You do not see any data for them in the *External System* *level* in *Schedules* or *Shift Center*.
- People who are scheduled for activities of type Vacation or Illness, and who are logged into the external system appear as Not Scheduled.
- People who are not scheduled but are engaged in an activity appear as Not scheduled.
- People who are not scheduled and are offline are not displayed.
====
The status *Out of adherence* has the following subtypes, which are color-coded in the xref:#adherence-overview-table[overview table]:

[%header, cols="1a,1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Subtype
|Color
|Description
|*Not present*
|Red
|The person is scheduled to work on a specific activity, but there is no information available from the external system. Data is not imported or not mapped correctly.
|*Wrong activity*
|Yellow
|According to the external system, the person is working on an activity that does not match the scheduled activity in Peopleware.
|*Not Scheduled*
|Blue
|The person is not scheduled for any activity in Peopleware but the external system sent a status mapped to an activity of type Presence, Break, or Meeting. This subtype does not negatively affect the *adherence score*. The person is not displayed at all if the activity type of the status sent is Absence, Illness, or Vacation.
|===

[#understand-the-real-time-adherence-page]
== Understand the *Real-Time Adherence* page
*Real-time adherence* displays an overview of your *people*'s adherence data in real time. Select at least a *planning unit* to display data. The page is divided into six sections, which are explained below.

[#real-time-adherence]
=== menu:Real-time[]
This section shows the percentage of *people* who are currently in adherence (including those within the configured xref:#buffer-time[*buffer time*]). This score refers only to the *people* from the selected *planning unit* and, if applicable, *selection*.

[#intraday-adherence]
=== Intraday adherence
This section shows the aggregate *adherence score* for the past intervals of the current day for the selected *planning unit* and/or *selection*. The graph shows the daily adherence trends.

Hover over the graph to see values for single intervals, color-coded as follows:

- Gray: *Adherence score* for the past intervals
- Blue: Daily trend of the *adherence score*
- Pink: The pink dashed line shows the configured xref:#target-adherence-score[*target adherence score*]. Additionally, adherence scores lower than the configured target score are highlighted in pink everywhere in the *Real-Time Adherence* page.

image::real-time-adherence/image-3.png[Intraday Adherence graphs, width=45%]

Click the section title to open a more detailed view on an independent tab. The detail view displays a table with a timeline for individual *people*. You can click a person's name in the table to open their data on an independent tab.

[NOTE]
====
Times are displayed in the xref:best-practices:working-with-timezones.adoc[time zone] of the selected *planning unit*. If you have only chosen a *selection*, and have planning units with different time zones, all times appear in the *time zone* configured in your user profile. The graphs display the xref:administration:create-and-manage-planning-units.adoc#add-business-hours[business hours] of the selected *planning unit* where applicable.
====
[#agent-overview]
=== Agent overview
This section lists how many *people* currently have the different *adherence statuses*. The colors of the statuses match those of the *people* rows highlighted in the table underneath.

Click a status to filter the table underneath by that status. Click the same status again to remove it from the filter. You can click several statuses to filter by them at the same time.

[#breaks-and-presence]
=== Breaks and Presence
This section shows data on how many *people* are working on their *scheduled activities*, divided into activities of type *Break* and *Presence*.

The data is structured as follows:

- Scheduled: Number of *people* scheduled for activities of type *Break* or *Presence*.
- Actual: Number of *people* with a status matching their *scheduled activity*.
- Difference: Number of *people* with a different status than their *scheduled activity*.

[#activity-overview]
=== Activity overview
In this section, you can see how many *people* are scheduled for an activity in Peopleware and how many *people* are currently working on this activity. You can sort the table by clicking any header.

Hover over an activity to display the {icon-magnifying-glass} and the {icon-matches}.

Click the {icon-magnifying-glass} to filter the overview table to the right by that activity. Click the icon again to remove that activity from the filter. You can filter by several activities at the same time.

Click the {icon-matches} to map and match external statuses to Peopleware activities. Learn more about xref:intraday:adherence-matches.adoc[activity matches].

[#adherence-overview-table]
=== Adherence overview table
The table shows the *adherence status* of all *people* assigned to the *planning unit* and/or *selection* you selected. Check the checkbox *Show only out of adherence* next to the search bar to show only *people* who are *out of adherence*.

Click any header to filter the table by that column. You can additionally filter the table by activity or status by clicking the relevant activities or statuses in the sections *Agent overview* and *Activity overview*.

The table contains the following columns:

[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Column
|Description
|Name
|Name of the person
|*Actual activity*
|Name of the activity that the person is currently working on (based on data from the External System *level*)
|Status
|Status as a colored marker with a tooltip showing the name of the status
|*Scheduled activity*
|Name of the activity for which the person is currently scheduled, if applicable
|*Out-of-adherence duration*
|Time the person has been *out of adherence* (including the xref:#buffer-time[*buffer time*])
|*Current activity duration*
|Time passed since the start of the current activity
|===

[#individual-adherence-details]
=== Individual adherence details
To see more detailed information on a specific person, click their name in the table. A timeline opens under the table showing their scheduled activities and *out-of-adherence statuses* of the current day. At the top right of the timeline, you can see their *adherence score* based on the total time the person has been in and *out of adherence* during that day.

image::real-time-adherence/image-6.png[Details of a person, width=100%]

Click the person's name in the timeline view to open the person's adherence data on an independent tab.

In the timeline, click a time period in the *Out of adherence* row to open a detailed view of the actual activities the person performed instead of the scheduled ones. You can also see the activity type and the duration. The detailed view groups short periods where a person was *out of adherence* in a row into a single block.

image::real-time-adherence/image-7.png[Out-of-adherence details, width=100%]

[#buffer-time]
== *Buffer time*
In a real work environment, *people* are rarely able to adhere to their schedule to the second, which can lead to a cluttered overview of *people* marked as *out of adherence*. Users with admin access can configure a *buffer time* for each *out-of-adherence subtype* that defines how long *people* are *out of adherence* before the status becomes effective. This helps you focus on the *people* who really need your attention.

If a person is within the defined *buffer time*, the time displayed in the *Out of adherence duration* column increases but the following applies:

- Their row is not highlighted. Only the status marker changes.
- Their status has no negative effect on the *adherence score*.
- Their row is not displayed when the *Show only out of adherence* filter is active.

To configure or edit the *buffer time*, follow these steps:

1. At the top right of the overview table, click the {icon-context-menu}.
2. Select *Adjust your buffer time*.
3. Select values for the *Not present*, *Wrong activity* and *Not scheduled* subtypes from the drop-down menus.
4. {btn-apply}

[NOTE]
====
The configured *buffer times* apply to everyone in your Peopleware tenant.
====
[#target-adherence-score]
== *Target adherence score*
You can configure a *target adherence score* to better identify *people* and intervals that fall below the target of your organization. This value is displayed as a pink dashed line in the adherence graphs. All percentage values below the target score are also highlighted in pink. By default, the *target adherence score* is set to 90%.

Users with admin access can edit the *target adherence score* by following these steps:

1. At the top right of the overview table, click the {icon-context-menu}.
2. Select *Adjust your target adherence score*.
3. Enter a new value.
4. {btn-apply}

[NOTE]
====
The configured target score applies to everyone in your Peopleware tenant.
====
[#troubleshoot-wrong-or-missing-external-status-information-in-peopleware]
== Troubleshoot wrong or missing external status information in Peopleware
People without an assigned *external identifier* appear as Offline, because Peopleware cannot import their status data. You also see no data for these *people* in the External System *level* in *Shift Center* or *Schedules*. To see status data for a person, assign them a correct *external identifier*, or check their assigned one for typos.

If you see unexpected gaps in the data in the External System *level* in *Shift Center*, make sure all relevant external activities are mapped to either the Present activity (ID 1) or other activities. Learn more about xref:intraday:map-external-status.adoc#map-external-statuses-to-activities-in-intraday[activity mapping].

If this does not resolve the problem, contact our support team. They will ask you to generate a report in your *external system* that shows the status data logged, and to provide screenshots of your Real-Time Adherence page and of the data in the External System *level* in *Shift Center* to track incoming statuses internally.
