In this article, you will learn how to allow employees to:

- participate in shift bidding.
- swap shifts with other employees.
- book time off.
- see one's own schedule.
- see the schedules of team members.
- set custom availabilities for certain time periods.

As a user with _Admin access_, you can turn Me features on or off. For this, go to **Me** in the main navigation and click the **button** for the respective feature.

[NOTE]
====
Employees need to get their Peopleware account to access Me and benefit from the features described. Learn how to xref:me:give-employees-access-to-me.adoc[create an account for them].
====
[#shift-bidding]
== Shift bidding
Employees can request shifts from a pool of shifts which have been created beforehand based on employee requirements and shift requirements. Shifts are then distributed in a lottery and remaining shifts can be assigned manually. Learn more about xref:scheduling:schedules-shift-bidding.adoc[shift bidding].

[#shift-swaps]
== Shift swaps
Employees can independently swap their shifts or days off with other employees. The process works as follows:

1. A shift is offered by an employee.
2. Another employee sees the offer and accepts it.
3. The shifts are swapped.

Shift swap auto-acceptance lets people select preferred shifts and, optionally, specific colleagues when offering a shift. When a response matches those preferences, Me accepts the swap automatically and notifies the person about the auto-approval.

Learn more about xref:scheduling:what-are-scheduling-periods.adoc#when-can-people-swap-shifts[how to allow your employees to swap shifts]. In the xref:#team-calendar[Team Calendar], employees can see the working hours of their team members. Depending on your configuration, you also need to xref:scheduling:view-approve-shift-swap-requests.adoc[approve pending swap requests].

[#time-off-requests]
== Time-off requests
Employees can request time off for xref:administration:activity-types-and-properties.adoc#activity-types[activities] of type Absence, Vacation, or Illness for periods ranging from a few hours of a single day up to several weeks. They can also check their annual vacation entitlement and the remaining vacation days.

Learn more about how to xref:scheduling:time-off-periods-legacy.adoc[activate time-off requests for your employees]. Manage the way employees can request time off with xref:scheduling:vacation-entitlement.adoc[vacation entitlement settings], xref:scheduling:vacation-approval-rules-and-quota-settings.adoc[approval rules] and xref:scheduling:vacation-approval-rules-and-quota-settings.adoc#quota-settings[quotas]. Approve or reject requests in xref:scheduling:vacation-absences-management.adoc[Time Off].

image::configure-me/image-1.png[Screen to enter a time-off request, width=50%]

[NOTE]
====
Only activities of type _vacation_ influence the vacation entitlement.
====
[#team-calendar]
== Team calendar
People can see the working hours of colleagues who are in the same xref:administration:selections.adoc[selection] in **Team Calendar**. If you created a scheduling period in any status other than Unpublished, the calendar shows presence times, breaks, and vacation days, but not the actual activity names. If there is no scheduling period or one with the Unpublished status, the team calendar does not show any information.

image::configure-me/image-7.png[Team calendar, width=80%]

[#availabilities]
== Availabilities
Employees can use the **Availabilities** menu item to set custom times when they are available or not available to work. They can set time frames of any length, independent from e.g. the hours defined in the contract. Learn more about scheduling using xref:scheduling:scheduling-methods.adoc#availabilities[availabilities].

If your employees enter time slots that are too short, parts of the shift schedule may not be created. Therefore, check which availabilities your employees have entered, in the last step before creating the schedule.

image::configure-me/image-2.png[Set availability, width=50%]

[#vacation-balance-in-hours]
== Vacation balance in hours
Changes the display of the vacation balance from days to hours. This applies to the requested, remaining and approved time off that is displayed to employees under the menu item **Time Off**.

This switch does not affect the display of daily and hourly values in other places, such as **Vacation/Absence Management** or **Vacation Entitlement**.
