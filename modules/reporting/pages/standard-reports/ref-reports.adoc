[#people]
== People
[#people-filter]
=== People filter
[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Name
|Description
|Planning units
|Filters the people list by planning unit.
|Selections
|Filters the people list by selection. Only works if people are assigned to at least one selection.
|Contracts
|Filters the people list by contract.
|===

=== Settings
[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]

|Name
|Description
|Level

[#format-delivery]
=== Format delivery
[%header, cols="1a,1a,".rstrip(',')]
|===
|Name
|Description
|Format
|Report format: PDF or HTML
|Send report to
|Defines how to email the report:  +- **Do not send**: Does not send the report. + - **Selected recipients**: Sends the report to the recipients defined in the report configuration. + - **Selected people (shared CC)**: Sends the report to the selected people, all CC’d on the same email thread. +- **Custom email address**: Sends the report to one or more manually entered email addresses.
|Export as anonymous report
|Hides personal identifiers such as people names and personnel numbers in the report.
|===
